# Ship it: ratio, export, caption, posting (Instagram first)

Facts dated 2026-10-29. Platform rules change: refresh anything older than six months, and say when something is unverified.

## Ratio
- Instagram Reels, TikTok, Shorts: **9:16, 1080x1920**. A 16:9 film fills only about 32 percent of a phone screen and shrinks every pixel to 56 percent. If your film is 16:9, put it on a laptop or phone screen inside a 9:16 frame (see `laptop-mockup.md`) or rebuild the scene at 1080x1920.
- YouTube and web: 16:9, 1920x1080.

## Export (Instagram)
MP4, H.264 High, yuv420p, BT.709, 1080x1920, 30 to 60 fps, VBR under 25 Mbps (14 to 20 is fine), AAC stereo 48 kHz, metadata at the front (`-movflags +faststart`), no edit list, 3 s to 3 min (Reels up to 3 minutes can reach non-followers), file under 300 MB. Whether Instagram serves 60 fps is unverified. A 40 s film at 16 Mbps is about 80 MB: size is not a reach problem.

## What Instagram says it ranks (relayed, dated)
- Reels are shown to a small audience first and widened by performance. Top named signals: watch time, likes, sends (read as ratios per reach).
- Originality is rewarded and copies lose eligibility; recency matters (a large share of impressions goes to posts under 24 hours old); small public accounts are "auditioned" to non-followers.
- Hashtags do not drive distribution, they help categorise: use 0 to 5 relevant ones. Irrelevant hashtags hurt. Keywords in caption, bio and the content help Search.
- AI content is not throttled but should be labelled. Turn on the in-app AI label for synthetic voices or visuals and say so in the caption. The exact label rules are not verified: read the toggle text.
- Engagement bait ("tag 3 friends", "comment YES to boost") can make a reel unrecommendable. A single-word comment trigger that unlocks a real deliverable (a repo, a guide) answered by private reply is allowed. One private reply per comment, within 7 days.
- Folklore, do not build on it: "the first hour decides", sends worth 3 to 5 times a like, shadowbans of normal accounts.

## Caption (plain, first person, no AI cadence)
Line 1: the surprising claim plus topic keywords. Line 2: what it does, with one real number. Line 3: the single call to action. Then the disclosure line (AI voice) and 3 to 5 relevant hashtags. No em dashes, no "unlock", "elevate", "game changer".

## Posting plan
1. Make the account public before posting (a private account's reels only reach approved followers).
2. Post when your audience is awake. Without your own Insights, the only published windows are Hootsuite averages in UTC (Mon 14:00 to 19:00, Tue 16:00 to 19:00, Thu 11:00 to 16:00); convert to your timezone and replace them with your own Insights after a few posts. Scheduling does not hurt reach.
3. Cover frame: the biggest number or the logo, inside the middle 75 percent of the frame height.
4. Stay online for the first hours and answer every comment; do the private replies by hand (free) or with a tool.
5. After 24 hours read the retention graph: where the viewers leave (first 3 seconds, the middle dip) is the brief for the next film. Do not repost the same reel repeatedly.
6. Do not promise reach. The studio thresholds are heuristics that you calibrate against real Insights.
